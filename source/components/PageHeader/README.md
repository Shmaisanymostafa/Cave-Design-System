The title row at the top of a product page: what this page is, and what you can do on it.

Provide the page title (`page-title`, or `ar-page-title` in Arabic), an optional one-line subtitle, and a toolbar with a search field, secondary actions and at most one primary action.

- Product titles use Poppins/Almarai SemiBold/Bold, not Hero. Hero is for brand moments (landing, sign-in, splash).
- The toolbar wraps below the title on narrow widths and the primary action stays last in reading order.
- Page title is the only `h1` on the page. Put filters in a row under the header, not inside it.
- Mirrors automatically in RTL: the title sits at the start edge, actions at the end.
