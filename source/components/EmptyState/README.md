What a list, table or page shows when it has nothing yet, and the placeholder shown while it loads.

Provide a headline that says what is empty, one sentence on why or what to do, and one action. For loading, put `cv-skeleton` bars where the text will land.

- Empty states teach: "No products yet", then the next step. Never "No data" or an apology.
- The mark is an outlined 28px icon from the line set on `accent-soft`; do not use illustrations with gradients or emoji.
- Use a skeleton for lists, tables and cards that load in under a few seconds; use the spinner only inside a button or for a full-screen wait.
- Skeletons pulse; with reduced motion they stay still.
- Three variants to write copy for: first use (invite the action), no results (offer to clear filters), error (say what failed and offer retry).
