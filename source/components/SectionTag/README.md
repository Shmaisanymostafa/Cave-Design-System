The section tab: a teal pill flush to the page edge, rounded at its free end, naming the section in Hero capitals.

Provide the label text (one or two words). Set it in `display` capitals with `heading-lg` (32px) or larger so that white on `teal` passes at 3.30:1; below 24px use `on-accent` (`navy`) text on `accent`, which passes at 4.55:1. The free end takes `radius-pill`; the other end runs off the edge of the surface. In right-to-left layouts mirror it: flush right, rounded at the left.

- One tab per section, placed at the left edge, either at the top of the section or at the bottom of the page, as in the deck's GOAL, SYMBOLS and LOGO slides.
- Do not use it as a button, and do not stack tabs.
